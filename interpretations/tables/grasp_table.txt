KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR PAPER
------------------------
1. DOMAIN GAP: Zero-shot transfer fails (PE_6h ≈ 0) because the GSAT-12R
   sensor suite differs from GOES-15 (15 vs 16 SW features). The input layer
   must be re-adapted for each satellite.
2. RAPID ADAPTATION: Frozen Transfer Learning (freeze physics encoder, train
   only input/output layers) recovers PE_6h = 0.564 in 25 epochs with just
   57K trainable parameters vs 343K for full fine-tune.
3. PRETRAINING IS ESSENTIAL FOR EXTREME EVENTS: 
   - Scratch training yields PE_hi = 0.473 (high-flux event accuracy)
   - Full TL from GOES yields PE_hi = 0.603 (+13.0 pp)
   - Frozen TL from GOES yields PE_hi = 0.689 (+21.6 pp)
   With only ~1 year of GRASP data, the model cannot learn solar storm
   physics from scratch. Pretraining on GOES-15 (5 years, 3x more storms)
   is strictly necessary for reliable extreme-event prediction.
4. FEW-SHOT EFFICIENCY: The frozen encoder reaches PE_6h = 0.638 with just
   50% of GRASP training data, demonstrating rapid cross-satellite adaptation
   suitable for newly deployed missions with short operational history.
